*Accepted for publication in npj Robotics.*

A modified version of the COAST (COaxially Aligned STeerable) guidewire robot that introduces a concentric push-pull mechanism, enabling active stiffness control to improve steerability and quick-connect usability during minimally invasive cardiovascular interventions. My contribution covered the electrical development of the actuation system, including PCB and circuit design and an analog force sensor for precise actuation and sensing.
